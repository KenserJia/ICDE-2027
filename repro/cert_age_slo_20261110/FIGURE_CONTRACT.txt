Core conclusion
Certification fraction, timely certified completion rate, and whole-query tail
feasibility are separate properties at the tested offered-load points.

Archetype quantitative grid. ICDE double-column, Python/matplotlib only,
7.1x2.25in, editable PDF/SVG plus600dpi TIFF/PNG preview. Minimum glyph5pt.
The panel for scheduled p99 has slightly more width as the feasibility test.

Panel a reports certification fraction G(.1), with median and full technical-run
range for every method/load point. It answers what fraction can be certified.
Panel b reports scheduled query p99 on a positive log axis with a20ms reference.
It answers whether all-query tail feasibility holds in each repeated run.
Panel c reports timely certified queries per second with the same aggregation.
It answers how many individually valid and timely completions the service yields.

Three methods, three shared load settings, three technical repeats per setting.
Use all27 primary observations. No population confidence intervals or p-values.
Methods have fixed colors and redundant marker shapes in every panel. Points
are grouped categorically by tested base/burst load; horizontal method offsets
distinguish matched conditions. Do not connect points or shade an interpolated
capacity region. The table separately reports alpha_max=min-repeat G only when
all3 p99 measurements meet20ms; this is a finite-horizon observed condition.

Source data is exported directly from complete load_records.json. Missing runs
block plotting. There are no microscopy images, external templates, synthetic
measurement values, smoothing, data exclusions or outlier removals. Source and
rendered-PDF font audits plus final-size panel/whole-figure visual checks follow.

Review risks
Three technical repeats are not independent deployments. Grouped categorical
positions must not be read as equally spaced numerical request rates. A high
timely-certified rate does not imply a p99 deadline guarantee. Same-age methods
can have overlapping quality; offsets must make that visible without exaggerating
differences. Span-certificate kernel timings and previous long runs are separate
experiments and do not enter this figure.
